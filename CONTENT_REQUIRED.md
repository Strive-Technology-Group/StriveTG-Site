# Owner Content Required Before Full Credibility Sign-off

Engineering work can be released only after the owner confirms the operational facts needed for the production configuration. The site is intentionally honest without the items below, but its market credibility will remain incomplete until approved facts and evidence are supplied.

Do not publish an item merely because it appears plausible. Record its source, owner approval, date approved, and any expiry or usage restrictions.

## 1. Founder identity and operating model

- Approved founder name, role/title, short biography, and long biography
- Current founder headshot, alt-text description, photographer/source, and publication permission
- Approved LinkedIn, GitHub, or other professional profile links
- Confirmation that “founder-led” is accurate
- Confirmation of whether outside specialists or contractors are used and approved wording for that model
- Any relevant experience claims, with dates and evidence; do not imply employer endorsement

## 2. Legal business identity

- Full legal entity name, entity type, jurisdiction, and any DBA/trade-name relationship
- Approved business mailing address and whether it may appear publicly
- Primary operating/service area
- Confirmation that `https://strivetg.com` is the canonical production domain
- Confirmation of any `www` redirect and ownership of every published domain
- Approved legal, privacy, billing, and general-contact email addresses
- Business telephone number, if one should be public
- Tax or registration details only if legally necessary and approved for publication

## 3. Evidence and social proof

For every proposed client, logo, testimonial, case study, or result:

- Written client permission and permitted attribution/anonymization
- Approved client name or anonymized identifier
- Initial problem and relevant baseline
- Contracted scope and what Strive actually delivered
- Project timeline with start/end context
- Measured result, data source, attribution limits, and measurement method
- Date range and factors outside Strive's control
- Approved quotation and speaker name/title, if applicable
- Logo/brand-use permission, source asset, and usage restrictions
- Review/expiry date for the published evidence

The `/work` page must remain method-focused until at least one complete, permissioned evidence package exists.

For every proposed documentary or project image, also provide the source file, subject/context, descriptive alt text, photographer or creator credit, usage permission, and any expiry or placement restriction. Do not substitute stock employees or generated portraits for missing company photography.

## 4. Commercial facts

- Approved representative pricing ranges or starting prices, if they can be honored
- Whether the fit call is free and the exact duration/format
- Typical timeline ranges by engagement type, supported by delivery history
- Deposit, milestone billing, late-fee, refund, cancellation, and change-order rules
- What is included/excluded from hosting, maintenance, monitoring, and support
- Whether separately licensed subscription products exist and how they differ from custom deliverables

## 5. Service and support operations

- Supported technologies/platforms Strive can actually maintain
- Business hours, service timezone, and observed holiday policy
- Approved response-time commitments by channel and service tier
- Incident, escalation, maintenance-window, backup, restoration, and monitoring practices
- Availability or uptime commitments only when contractually and operationally supportable
- Data export, access handoff, domain transfer, hosting transition, and offboarding procedures
- A named owner for the contact inbox and Calendly calendar

## 6. Privacy, security, and data handling facts

- Production hosting provider, region, logging behavior, and retention settings
- Email provider and retention/deletion practice for inquiries
- Calendly account owner, configured event URL, retention settings, and subprocessors
- Any analytics, error monitoring, session replay, advertising, CRM, or cookie tooling planned for production
- Categories of personal data processed, purpose, lawful basis where applicable, retention, deletion, and request process
- Whether international transfers, sensitive data, children, regulated data, or automated decisions are in scope
- Security practices that may be described publicly, with current evidence and a review owner
- Security or compliance certifications only when current, in-scope, and approved for this entity

## 7. External profiles and structured data

- Approved company profiles for `sameAs` structured data
- Verified social handles, directory profiles, and map/business listings
- Local-business address/hours/telephone only if accurate and approved
- Approved social-sharing image and any brand/legal constraints

## Publication rule

Keep unsupported fields absent—not marked “coming soon.” Before publishing new proof, have one person verify the source and another approve the exact public wording. Re-run the content, structured-data, accessibility, link, and visual checks after additions.

# Legal Review Required

The `/privacy` and `/terms` pages use client-facing language that reflects the current website behavior and intended engagement model. They are not yet approved production policies. Qualified counsel and the business owner must review them before launch.

`STRIVE_OFFER_AND_PRICING_STRATEGY.md` is the canonical commercial-strategy source. This document identifies legal-review questions and must not become a competing statement of commercial policy. `OWNER_LAUNCH_DECISIONS.md` records the concise initial-launch approvals.

## Policy changes made in this redesign

- Replaced generic or contradictory policy copy with concise, plain-language website disclosures.
- Described the form-first project brief, direct email fallback, and conditional approved business telephone path.
- Described HubSpot-confirmed durable delivery, transactional notifications, form fields, rate limiting, duplicate prevention, failure logging, and sensitive-data restrictions.
- Avoided claiming a first-party form, analytics tracker, advertising technology, or cookie behavior that is not implemented.
- Aligned ownership language across services, FAQ, and terms around categories the agreement must define: client materials and accounts, custom deliverables, reusable Strive materials, third-party licenses, access, and transfer responsibilities. The exact ownership and license rules are not approved yet.
- Added working commercial direction that separates initial projects, eligible ongoing monthly services, and expanded work. This direction is not a final contract or legal policy.
- Added a qualified month-to-month cancellation direction while leaving the active billing period, committed costs, refund treatment, transfer conditions, and offboarding mechanics to the approved agreement.
- Removed universal warranties, response promises, result guarantees, and unsupported compliance language.

## Decisions counsel and the owner must resolve

### Business identity and governing terms

- Exact legal entity, entity type, DBA relationship, notice address, and contact roles
- Governing law, venue, dispute process, and any arbitration/class-action provisions
- Age/eligibility requirements and territory-specific obligations
- Effective date, versioning, change notice, and policy acceptance process

### Privacy

- Controller/business/operator identity and all legally required disclosures
- Applicable jurisdictions and lawful bases, consent requirements, and user rights
- Complete data inventory for hosting logs, email, lead intake, future private scheduling, analytics, monitoring, CRM, and advertising tools
- Production processors/subprocessors, data locations, transfer mechanisms, and contractual safeguards
- Retention schedules, deletion routines, backup behavior, access controls, and request verification
- Cookie consent or opt-out obligations if future tooling changes runtime behavior
- Treatment of sensitive information, minors' data, regulated data, and automated decision-making
- Sale/share/targeted-advertising statements and required regional links or signals
- Security-incident and regulatory-contact language

### Commercial terms

- Proposal/order-of-precedence rules and when a contract is formed
- Fees, taxes, deposits, milestones, expenses, late payments, refunds, cancellation, and change control
- Delivery estimates, client delays, acceptance criteria, and dependencies
- Which services are eligible for month-to-month treatment and which remain fixed-scope projects
- Recurring authorization, material disclosures, billing frequency, renewal date, cancellation method and cutoff, effective date, confirmation, active-period treatment, and proration/refund rules
- Applicable federal and state automatic-renewal or negative-option requirements, including whether business-to-business or consumer rules apply to Strive's offers and clients
- Hosting, domain, maintenance, monitoring, support, renewal, failed payment, suspension, and termination terms
- Export format, transition assistance, cost, timing, and post-termination data handling
- Third-party software, open-source components, subscriptions, licenses, and pass-through terms
- Confidentiality, publicity/client-name permission, and case-study permission

### Intellectual property

- Precise definition of client materials, custom deliverables, Strive background IP, reusable tools, and licensed products
- Transfer timing after full payment and any license needed for embedded background IP
- Domain, account, credential, data, source-code, design-file, and documentation ownership
- Moral rights, portfolio rights, feedback, infringement procedure, and third-party assets

### Risk allocation

- Warranties and disclaimers
- Limitation of liability, liability caps, excluded damages, indemnities, and insurance requirements
- Security/privacy responsibilities and incident handling
- Accessibility, performance, SEO, availability, monitoring, backup, and business-result disclaimers
- Force majeure and continuity obligations

## Production hold

Do not describe these pages as final policies or rely on them as the sole project agreement. Before deployment, counsel should approve the exact production text, the owner should confirm that operations match it, and the team should retest links and runtime behavior after any legal edits.

The working public direction currently says that eligible ongoing monthly services do not require a long-term commitment, may be canceled before the next renewal, and stop future recurring charges according to the agreement. Counsel and the owner must approve that statement, the cancellation workflow, the agreement template, the billing configuration, and the treatment of the active period before production publication.

The FTC's 2024 amended Negative Option Rule was vacated by the Eighth Circuit in July 2025 and formally withdrawn by the FTC in February 2026. Do not present that withdrawn rule as controlling law. Counsel must assess the current federal and state rules that apply to Strive's jurisdictions, client types, and billing model.
